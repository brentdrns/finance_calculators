About Age Grading

WMA (World Masters Athletics) age grading uses performance factors derived from world records at each age to convert any runner's time to a percentage of the theoretical best for their age and sex.

A score of 100% equals the world record for your age group. Scores above 80% are considered national class; above 70% is regional class.

Factors are from the WMA 2015 road running tables.

For informational and educational purposes only. Age grading factors are based on WMA 2015 road running tables and are approximations. Results are estimates only.
