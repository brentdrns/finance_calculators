About This Data

Percentiles are based on aggregate U.S. road race finish time distributions from RunningUSA and published race result studies. Data reflects the full finisher field, not just age group leaders.

A higher percentile means a faster time — 90th percentile means you finished faster than 90% of all finishers in that event.

For informational and educational purposes only. Percentile data is based on aggregate U.S. road race finish time distributions and represents approximations — individual race fields vary significantly by event, location, and year.
