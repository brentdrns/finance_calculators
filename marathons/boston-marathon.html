Course Overview

The Boston Marathon course climbs a total of 660 ft202 m and descends 1,090 ft331 m, finishing 424 ft129 m lower than it starts. With about 25 ftabout 4.8 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 9th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Net downhill overall, but famous for rolling hills and Heartbreak Hill at mile 20.

The first half climbs 254 ft77 m and drops 564 ft172 m. The second half climbs 405 ft123 m and drops 517 ft158 m.

How to Pace the Boston Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:52/mi4:48/km to 8:14/mi5:08/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 20.3–20.6km 32.7–33.2 (+71 ft+22 m at 4.4%), late in the race when it hurts most. Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Don’t bank time on the early descent (miles 0.0–0.6km 0.0–1.0, −125 ft−38 m). Running it much faster than the plan costs your quads later in the race.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:46 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:44 for 4:00. That leaves a slower second half of about 1:45:14, because the second half has more climbing.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Boston Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 660 ft and descends 1,090 ft over the full marathon, about 25 ft of climbing per mile. That makes it the 9th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Boston Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at miles 16.1–16.4 (+43 ft at 2.6%); miles 17.5–17.8 (+59 ft at 3.6%); miles 20.3–20.6 (+71 ft at 4.4%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Boston Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 9:09 per mile. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the mile splits range from 9:00 to 9:25, and halfway comes at about 1:59:44.

Is the Boston Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 424 ft lower than the start (459 ft at the start, 35 ft at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Boston Marathon course?

The high point is 459 ft above sea level, at the start. The low point is 27 ft, at mile 25.5.
