Course Overview

The NYC Marathon course climbs a total of 790 ft240 m and descends 840 ft255 m, finishing 49 ft15 m lower than it starts. With about 30 ftabout 5.7 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 6th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Five bridge crossings and rolling pavement. The back half is harder than it looks.

The first half climbs 392 ft120 m and drops 467 ft142 m. The second half climbs 393 ft120 m and drops 367 ft112 m.

Where the Hills Are

The biggest climbs, in race order:

  1. Miles 0.0–0.6km 0.0–1.0: +104 ft+32 m over 0.61.0 mikm, averaging 3.2%.

  3. Miles 22.9–23.6km 36.8–38.0: +78 ft+24 m over 0.71.2 mikm, averaging 2.1%.

The biggest descents: miles 0.9–1.9km 1.5–3.1 (−189 ft−58 m); miles 15.6–16.1km 25.1–25.9 (−84 ft−26 m).

How to Pace the NYC Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:43/mi4:47/km to 8:16/mi5:14/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 14.6–15.3km 23.4–24.6 (+113 ft+35 m at 3.0%), late in the race when it hurts most. Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Don’t bank time on the early descent (miles 0.9–1.9km 1.5–3.1, −189 ft−58 m). Running it much faster than the plan costs your quads later in the race.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:55 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:55 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the NYC Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 790 ft and descends 840 ft over the full marathon, about 30 ft of climbing per mile. That makes it the 6th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the NYC Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at miles 0.0–0.6 (+104 ft at 3.2%); miles 14.6–15.3 (+113 ft at 3.0%); miles 22.9–23.6 (+78 ft at 2.1%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour NYC Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 9:09 per mile. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the mile splits range from 8:49 to 9:27, and halfway comes at about 1:59:55.

Is the NYC Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 49 ft lower than the start (156 ft at the start, 107 ft at the finish).

What is the highest point on the NYC Marathon course?

The high point is 271 ft above sea level, 0.8 miles into the race. The low point is 40 ft, at mile 11.8.
