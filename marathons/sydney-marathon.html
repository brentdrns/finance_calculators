Course Overview

The Sydney Marathon course climbs a total of 740 ft226 m and descends 1,030 ft315 m, finishing 292 ft89 m lower than it starts. With about 28 ftabout 5.3 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 7th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Crosses the Harbour Bridge early and finishes at the Opera House. Late-August conditions are usually mild.

The first half climbs 426 ft130 m and drops 647 ft197 m. The second half climbs 312 ft95 m and drops 382 ft117 m.

How to Pace the Sydney Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:49/mi4:47/km to 8:12/mi5:07/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 9.1–9.6km 14.7–15.5 (+68 ft+21 m at 2.5%). Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Don’t bank time on the early descent (miles 0.0–0.7km 0.0–1.2, −167 ft−51 m). Running it much faster than the plan costs your quads later in the race.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:59 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:58 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Sydney Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 226 m and descends 315 m over the full marathon, about 5.3 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 7th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Sydney Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at km 14.7–15.5 (+21 m at 2.5%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Sydney Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:28 to 5:51, and halfway comes at about 1:59:58.

Is the Sydney Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 89 m lower than the start (99 m at the start, 10 m at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Sydney Marathon course?

The high point is 99 m above sea level, at the start. The low point is 8 m, at km 7.0.
