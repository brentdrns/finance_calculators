How to Pace the Mumbai Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:54/mi4:51/km to 8:11/mi5:12/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 21.9–22.4km 35.3–36.0 (+73 ft+22 m at 3.4%), late in the race when it hurts most. Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Halfway: about 1:45:00 for a 3:30 finish and 2:00:00 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mumbai Marathon hilly?

Somewhat. It is rolling. The course climbs 129 m and descends 129 m over the full marathon, about 3.1 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 21st hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Mumbai Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at km 9.7–10.4 (+19 m at 2.9%); km 35.3–36.0 (+22 m at 3.4%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Mumbai Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:32 to 5:57, and halfway comes at about 2:00:00.

Is the Mumbai Marathon course net downhill?

No. It finishes within 0 m of its starting elevation.

What is the highest point on the Mumbai Marathon course?

The high point is 36 m above sea level, 36.1 km into the race. The low point is 1 m, at km 26.7.
