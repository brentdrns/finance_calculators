Course Overview

The Mexico City Marathon course climbs a total of 310 ft94 m and descends 470 ft144 m, finishing 166 ft51 m lower than it starts. With about 12 ftabout 2.2 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 20th flattest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 154 ft47 m and drops 230 ft70 m. The second half climbs 153 ft47 m and drops 244 ft74 m.

Where the Hills Are

There are no real hills on this course. No stretch climbs more than 39 ft12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way. The short rises that do exist are folded into the splits below.

How to Pace the Mexico City Marathon

  • Even pacing works here. For a 3:30 goal every milekilometer stays within 6 seconds5 seconds of the 8:01/mi4:59/km average, so a single goal pace on your watch is close enough.
  • Halfway: about 1:45:01 for a 3:30 finish and 2:00:01 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Altitude: the whole course sits above 7,300 ft2,200 m. Thin air slows most runners from sea level by several percent, on top of the hills. These splits don’t include that, so start more conservatively.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mexico City Marathon hilly?

Not very. It is mostly flat. The course climbs 94 m and descends 144 m over the full marathon, about 2.2 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 20th flattest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Mexico City Marathon course?

There are no real hills. No stretch of the course climbs more than 12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way.

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Mexico City Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:36 to 5:47, and halfway comes at about 2:00:01.

Is the Mexico City Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 51 m lower than the start (2,290 m at the start, 2,240 m at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Mexico City Marathon course?

The high point is 2,293 m above sea level, at the start. The low point is 2,238 m, at km 40.4.
