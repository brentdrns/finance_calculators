Course Overview

The Hamburg Marathon course climbs a total of 400 ft122 m and descends 400 ft121 m, finishing within 1 ft0 m of where it starts. With about 15 ftabout 2.9 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 23rd hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 215 ft66 m and drops 253 ft77 m. The second half climbs 183 ft56 m and drops 144 ft44 m.

Where the Hills Are

There are no real hills on this course. No stretch climbs more than 39 ft12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way. The short rises that do exist are folded into the splits below.

The biggest descents: miles 5.9–6.4km 9.6–10.2 (−71 ft−22 m).

How to Pace the Hamburg Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:55/mi4:53/km to 8:08/mi5:06/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • Don’t bank time on the early descent (miles 5.9–6.4km 9.6–10.2, −71 ft−22 m). Running it much faster than the plan costs your quads later in the race.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:57 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:57 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Hamburg Marathon hilly?

Somewhat. It is rolling. The course climbs 122 m and descends 121 m over the full marathon, about 2.9 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 23rd hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Hamburg Marathon course?

There are no real hills. No stretch of the course climbs more than 12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way.

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Hamburg Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:35 to 5:50, and halfway comes at about 1:59:57.

Is the Hamburg Marathon course net downhill?

No. It finishes within 0 m of its starting elevation.

What is the highest point on the Hamburg Marathon course?

The high point is 38 m above sea level, 5.0 km into the race. The low point is 3 m, at km 11.7.
