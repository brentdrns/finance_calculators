Course Overview

The Athens Marathon course climbs a total of 1,020 ft311 m and descends 830 ft252 m, finishing 193 ft59 m higher than it starts. With about 39 ftabout 7.4 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 3rd hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 505 ft154 m and drops 309 ft94 m. The second half climbs 515 ft157 m and drops 518 ft158 m.

How to Pace the Athens Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:44/mi4:50/km to 8:19/mi5:12/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 12.0–13.2km 19.3–21.3 (+166 ft+51 m at 2.5%). Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Don’t bank time on the early descent (miles 10.3–10.9km 16.5–17.5, −147 ft−45 m). Running it much faster than the plan costs your quads later in the race.
  • Halfway: about 1:45:10 for a 3:30 finish and 2:00:11 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Athens Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 311 m and descends 252 m over the full marathon, about 7.4 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 3rd hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Athens Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at km 10.9–11.4 (+13 m at 2.6%); km 15.5–16.0 (+18 m at 3.6%); km 19.3–21.3 (+51 m at 2.5%); km 24.1–25.4 (+36 m at 2.7%); km 30.0–31.2 (+34 m at 3.0%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Athens Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:32 to 5:57, and halfway comes at about 2:00:11.

Is the Athens Marathon course net downhill?

No, it is net uphill. The finish is 59 m higher than the start (36 m at the start, 95 m at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Athens Marathon course?

The high point is 246 m above sea level, 31.2 km into the race. The low point is 9 m, at km 7.5.
