Course Overview

The San Francisco Marathon course climbs a total of 1,180 ft361 m and descends 1,180 ft360 m, finishing within 5 ft2 m of where it starts. With about 45 ftabout 8.6 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 2nd hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Very hilly. Crosses the Golden Gate Bridge. Significant total elevation and morning fog.

The first half climbs 658 ft201 m and drops 487 ft148 m. The second half climbs 523 ft160 m and drops 689 ft210 m.

How to Pace the San Francisco Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:45/mi4:44/km to 8:25/mi5:36/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 5.8–7.0km 9.4–11.2 (+221 ft+67 m at 3.7%). Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Halfway: about 1:45:25 for a 3:30 finish and 2:00:29 for 4:00. That leaves a faster second half of about 1:44:35, because the first half has more climbing.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the San Francisco Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 1,180 ft and descends 1,180 ft over the full marathon, about 45 ft of climbing per mile. That makes it the 2nd hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the San Francisco Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at miles 5.8–7.0 (+221 ft at 3.7%); miles 10.8–11.4 (+217 ft at 6.7%); miles 13.1–13.7 (+93 ft at 2.7%); miles 16.0–16.4 (+80 ft at 3.7%); miles 22.0–22.4 (+42 ft at 2.5%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour San Francisco Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 9:09 per mile. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the mile splits range from 8:52 to 9:37, and halfway comes at about 2:00:29.

Is the San Francisco Marathon course net downhill?

No. It finishes within 5 ft of its starting elevation.

What is the highest point on the San Francisco Marathon course?

The high point is 312 ft above sea level, 17.7 miles into the race. The low point is 13 ft, at mile 4.1.
