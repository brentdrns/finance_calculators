Course Overview

The Istanbul Marathon course climbs a total of 580 ft178 m and descends 610 ft187 m, finishing within 30 ft9 m of where it starts. With about 22 ftabout 4.2 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 14th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 329 ft100 m and drops 460 ft140 m. The second half climbs 254 ft77 m and drops 152 ft46 m.

Where the Hills Are

The biggest climbs, in race order:

  1. Miles 0.2–0.5km 0.3–0.8: +45 ft+14 m over 0.30.5 mikm, averaging 2.8%.
  2. Miles 1.5–2.4km 2.5–3.8: +135 ft+41 m over 0.81.3 mikm, averaging 3.1%.
  3. Miles 25.2–25.4km 40.6–40.9: +39 ft+12 m over 0.20.3 mikm, averaging 3.6%.

The biggest descents: miles 1.0–1.3km 1.6–2.1 (−48 ft−15 m); miles 2.7–3.3km 4.3–5.3 (−230 ft−70 m).

How to Pace the Istanbul Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:51/mi4:44/km to 8:15/mi5:10/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 1.5–2.4km 2.5–3.8 (+135 ft+41 m at 3.1%) in the first few miles, so it is easy to overcook. Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Don’t bank time on the early descent (miles 2.7–3.3km 4.3–5.3, −230 ft−70 m). Running it much faster than the plan costs your quads later in the race.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:55 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:54 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Istanbul Marathon hilly?

Somewhat. It is rolling. The course climbs 178 m and descends 187 m over the full marathon, about 4.2 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 14th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Istanbul Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at km 0.3–0.8 (+14 m at 2.8%); km 2.5–3.8 (+41 m at 3.1%); km 40.6–40.9 (+12 m at 3.6%); km 41.4–42.2 (+25 m at 3.2%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Istanbul Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:25 to 5:55, and halfway comes at about 1:59:54.

Is the Istanbul Marathon course net downhill?

No. It finishes within 9 m of its starting elevation.

What is the highest point on the Istanbul Marathon course?

The high point is 90 m above sea level, 3.9 km into the race. The low point is 0 m, at km 36.6.
