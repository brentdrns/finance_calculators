Course Overview

The Madrid Marathon course climbs a total of 1,000 ft305 m and descends 1,090 ft333 m, finishing 93 ft28 m lower than it starts. With about 38 ftabout 7.2 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 4th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 456 ft139 m and drops 561 ft171 m. The second half climbs 542 ft165 m and drops 530 ft161 m.

Where the Hills Are

The biggest climbs, in race order:

  1. Miles 13.3–13.9km 21.4–22.4: +72 ft+22 m over 0.61.0 mikm, averaging 2.1%.
  2. Miles 16.6–16.9km 26.7–27.2: +41 ft+13 m over 0.30.5 mikm, averaging 2.5%.

The biggest descents: miles 11.2–11.5km 18.0–18.5 (−65 ft−20 m); miles 13.9–15.1km 22.4–24.3 (−233 ft−71 m); miles 20.6–21.1km 33.2–34.0 (−99 ft−30 m).

How to Pace the Madrid Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:41/mi4:46/km to 8:14/mi5:07/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 13.3–13.9km 21.4–22.4 (+72 ft+22 m at 2.1%). Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:50 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:49 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Madrid Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 305 m and descends 333 m over the full marathon, about 7.2 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 4th hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Madrid Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at km 21.4–22.4 (+22 m at 2.1%); km 26.7–27.2 (+13 m at 2.5%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Madrid Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:27 to 5:51, and halfway comes at about 1:59:49.

Is the Madrid Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 28 m lower than the start (679 m at the start, 650 m at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Madrid Marathon course?

The high point is 740 m above sea level, 3.7 km into the race. The low point is 581 m, at km 36.2.
