Course Overview

The Barcelona Marathon course climbs a total of 290 ft89 m and descends 340 ft104 m, finishing 50 ft15 m lower than it starts. With about 11 ftabout 2.1 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 18th flattest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 177 ft54 m and drops 193 ft59 m. The second half climbs 113 ft34 m and drops 147 ft45 m.

Where the Hills Are

There are no real hills on this course. No stretch climbs more than 39 ft12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way. The short rises that do exist are folded into the splits below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Barcelona Marathon hilly?

Not very. It is mostly flat. The course climbs 89 m and descends 104 m over the full marathon, about 2.1 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 18th flattest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Barcelona Marathon course?

There are no real hills. No stretch of the course climbs more than 12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way.

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Barcelona Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:37 to 5:45, and halfway comes at about 2:00:03.

Is the Barcelona Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 15 m lower than the start (33 m at the start, 18 m at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Barcelona Marathon course?

The high point is 47 m above sea level, 15.6 km into the race. The low point is 5 m, at km 32.3.
