Course Overview

The Big Sur Marathon course climbs a total of 1,520 ft462 m and descends 1,840 ft561 m, finishing 324 ft99 m lower than it starts. With about 58 ftabout 11.0 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Big Sur to Carmel along Highway 1, with the long climb to Hurricane Point around miles 10–12. One of the most scenic and challenging marathons.

The first half climbs 842 ft257 m and drops 918 ft280 m. The second half climbs 670 ft204 m and drops 918 ft280 m.

How to Pace the Big Sur Marathon

  • Expect your pace to move. For a 3:30 goal the plan runs anywhere from 7:36/mi4:45/km to 8:49/mi5:31/km, around an average of 8:01/mi4:59/km. Pacing to the average on every milekilometer would mean working harder on the climbs and wasting the descents.
  • The biggest climb is miles 9.7–12.0km 15.7–19.3 (+504 ft+154 m at 4.2%). Hold your effort steady up it and let the pace slow; you get some of the time back on the far side.
  • Halfway: about 1:45:16 for a 3:30 finish and 2:00:18 for 4:00. That leaves a faster second half of about 1:44:44, because the first half has more climbing.
  • Weather often matters more than hills. The Race Time Predictor adjusts your goal for race-day temperature and humidity as well as this course’s elevation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Big Sur Marathon hilly?

Yes, it is hilly. The course climbs 1,520 ft and descends 1,840 ft over the full marathon, about 58 ft of climbing per mile. That makes it the hilliest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Big Sur Marathon course?

The biggest climbs are at miles 8.2–8.7 (+82 ft at 3.0%); miles 9.7–12.0 (+504 ft at 4.2%); miles 13.8–14.6 (+88 ft at 2.2%); miles 15.1–15.6 (+83 ft at 3.0%); miles 21.5–22.2 (+129 ft at 3.3%).

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Big Sur Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 9:09 per mile. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the mile splits range from 8:42 to 10:04, and halfway comes at about 2:00:18.

Is the Big Sur Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 324 ft lower than the start (358 ft at the start, 34 ft at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Big Sur Marathon course?

The high point is 562 ft above sea level, 11.9 miles into the race. The low point is 34 ft, at mile 24.9.
