Course Overview

The Seoul Marathon course climbs a total of 280 ft85 m and descends 340 ft105 m, finishing 66 ft20 m lower than it starts. With about 11 ftabout 2.0 m of climbing per milekilometer, it’s the 17th flattest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

The first half climbs 117 ft36 m and drops 152 ft46 m. The second half climbs 161 ft49 m and drops 192 ft58 m.

Where the Hills Are

There are no real hills on this course. No stretch climbs more than 39 ft12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way. The short rises that do exist are folded into the splits below.

How to Pace the Seoul Marathon

  • Even pacing works here. For a 3:30 goal every milekilometer stays within 5 seconds of the 8:01/mi4:59/km average, so a single goal pace on your watch is close enough.
  • Halfway: about 1:44:58 for a 3:30 finish and 1:59:58 for 4:00. The halves are close to even on this course.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Seoul Marathon hilly?

Not very. It is mostly flat. The course climbs 85 m and descends 105 m over the full marathon, about 2.0 m of climbing per km. That makes it the 17th flattest of the 49 marathons in our course library.

Where are the hills on the Seoul Marathon course?

There are no real hills. No stretch of the course climbs more than 12 m, so you can run close to even pace the whole way.

What pace do I need for a 4-hour Seoul Marathon?

A 4:00 marathon averages 5:41 per kilometer. Adjusted for this course at 60% hill effort, the kilometer splits range from 5:38 to 5:47, and halfway comes at about 1:59:58.

Is the Seoul Marathon course net downhill?

Yes, it is net downhill. The finish is 20 m lower than the start (34 m at the start, 14 m at the finish).

What is the highest point on the Seoul Marathon course?

The high point is 40 m above sea level, 1.3 km into the race. The low point is 9 m, at km 39.7.
